# Transcript audit sheet — what your Esprit transcript must prove

The binding constraint across most of this dataset is **not** total credits. It is
**named subject credits**, and a Business Intelligence degree from a business school
can fail these while holding far more than 180 ECTS in total.

Take your transcript, and for each row below count the ECTS you actually hold in that
category. Do this **before** spending effort on applications — a failure here is
silent, and it surfaces after you have paid an application fee.

## Corvinus University of Budapest — the most precisely documented in the dataset

Credit recognition is **mandatory** for every holder of a foreign bachelor. Source:
Corvinus 2026 credit-recognition table ("honnan-hova tábla").

| Programme | Full requirement | Absolute entry floor | Categories you must evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Business Informatics** | 70 ECTS | **40 ECTS** | Natural Sciences 10 · Business & HR Management 20 · **Informatics 40** |
| **Artificial Intelligence in Business** | 36 ECTS | **24 ECTS** | Methodology 12 (max 6 catch-up) · Programming, Python or R 12 (max 6 catch-up) · Economics — business economics, accounting, finance, marketing, management 12 (all catch-up-able) |
| **Social Data Science** | 36 ECTS | **30 ECTS** | Methodology 12 — **cannot be caught up** · Programming 12 — **cannot be caught up** · Economics or Social Sciences 12 (6 catch-up-able) |
| **Communication and Media Studies** | 30 ECTS | **30 ECTS** | One pooled 30 ECTS across business, communication, HR, marketing, media theory, sociology, social psychology, PR — **no catch-up allowance at all** |

**Read this carefully:** *Social Data Science* is the sharpest risk in the whole
dataset. Its 12 ECTS of methodology and 12 ECTS of programming must already be on your
transcript and **cannot be made up after admission**. *Business Informatics* wants
**40 ECTS of named informatics courses**, which a business-school BI degree may simply
not carry, however technical it felt to study.

## Erasmus Mundus — checked before your application is scored

| Programme | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **EMAI** | 12 ECTS each of mathematics, programming and computing science. Accepts a non-ICT 180-ECTS bachelor with 10–60 ECTS of bridging. |
| **DEAI** | Accepts a 180-ECTS bachelor. Its rubric **awards points per year of work in an IT or BI position** — your work history is scored, not just tolerated. |

## Other named prerequisites

| Institution | What must be on the transcript |
|---|---|
| **Vilnius — Digital Marketing** | 20 ECTS in Business/Public Management or Economics, plus a 70% grade average |
| **Vilnius — Strategic Management of IS** | 20 ECTS in microeconomics, macroeconomics or econometrics (or 3 years' verified work experience), plus 60% average |
| **Tilburg / TU Eindhoven JADS** | 15 EC mathematics and statistics (min 5 EC each), plus Databases, Data Structures and Algorithms, Programming (preferably Python), Machine Learning / Data Mining |
| **Algebra — Economics of Digital Business** | 180 ECTS; if the degree is not in Economics you need "at least a few basic economics courses", else a **free** online preparatory programme is assigned (can be taken after enrolment) |
| **Algebra — Data Science / Digital Marketing** | 180+ ECTS in technical, natural or social sciences; without prior technical/CS/social-science study a preparatory programme is assigned that is **charged on top of tuition** and normally must be completed **before** enrolment |
| **UTM Moldova — Data Science** | Numerical Methods, Data Structures and Algorithms, OOP, Procedural Programming, Python, Human-Computer Interaction. Different-field candidates complete prerequisites before admission or via bridging modules |
| **AUTH — Digital Media, Computational Intelligence** | A degree in computer science, electrical/computer engineering or a numerate physical science — a real risk for a BI licence |
| **UCC — MA Film & Screen Media** | The honours degree must be **"in the Humanities"**. A BI licence fails on subject before credits are considered |

## Grade floors and administrative gates

| Institution | Requirement |
|---|---|

| Tallinn University | At least **60%** of the possible maximum |
| Vilnius | 70% (Digital Marketing) / 60% (Strategic Management) |
| Romania (state scheme) | 7/10 |
| IE Dean's Award | GPA ≥ 3.8/4 (9.5/10) |
| **PJAIT, Poland** | A Tunisian diploma needs a written **NAWA Director decision** confirming eligibility to continue studies, plus apostille. Tunisia is on no exemption list. |
| **FAMU, Czechia** | Foreign-diploma **nostrification** must be completed to enrol in September. This, not credits, is the real gate. |
| **Estonian Academy of Arts** | Tunisia is **not listed** on its country-requirements page — email admissions@artun.ee for the document set. |
| **Greece (all)** | DOATAP / Hellenic NARIC recognition of the foreign title. At AUTH DMCI, recognition is a condition of **being awarded the master's**, not merely of entry. |

## Recency rules — these behave as hard cutoffs

| Institution | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Vilnius University** | For non-EU applicants, the gap since the bachelor **must not exceed five years** |
| **ASE Bucharest (FABIZ)** | Non-EU applicants must have graduated **2023 or later** |

E4 checked its whole territory for further recency rules and found none, so these two
appear to be the exceptions rather than a regional pattern.
